About WYMIS

Built so graduates leave ready.

WYMIS was created by Dr. Barry K. Jackson, Ph.D. on a simple idea: a diploma should come with the practical confidence to use it.

The founder

Dr. Barry K. Jackson, Ph.D.

Dr. Barry K. Jackson has built his career in workforce and youth development, connecting students and adult learners with employers and careers.

He is a Senior Career Services Advisor at Bryan University in Tempe, Arizona, where he builds employer relationships that move graduates into jobs and prepares students for career entry. He previously served as a Director of Programs with the National Urban League and as Executive Director of The Memphis Youth Coalition. He holds a Master of Divinity and has training in pastoral counseling.

Dr. Jackson created WYMIS to close the gap between a high school diploma and the practical skills adult life demands. He built it as a cohort-based, skills-first program and piloted it with students in Phoenix, Las Vegas, and Memphis, refining the curriculum from real classroom experience and feedback.

The Vision

Students deserve to graduate not just with a diploma, but with the practical confidence to manage their money, present themselves professionally, build relationships, and start something of their own if they choose to.

What guides WYMIS

Practical, shared, flexible, and built to grow.

Practical first

Real situations

Every module is built around situations students will face immediately after graduation.

Learn together

Cohort model

Students progress as a group, creating accountability and peer support.

Flexible

Two formats

A 75-minute session for a focused introduction, or a 3-day unit for real practice.

Built to grow

Licensed model

The goal is a repeatable model schools and organizations can bring to their own communities.
