Privacy policy

How WYMIS collects, uses, and protects information on this website. Last updated October 21, 2026.

WYMIS (What You Missed In School), a program under the umbrella of Love Nation Inc., a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, operates this website. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you send an inquiry through the Partner page, we collect what you enter: your name, organization, email address, phone number (optional), the type of organization you represent, the program format you are interested in, and your message.

When you register for a One-Day Intensive, we collect the event you chose, your name, email address, phone number (optional), age range, school or organization (optional), how you heard about WYMIS (optional), whether you request the $100 senior offer (where offered), and any accessibility needs or questions you share. If you are under 18, we also collect a parent or guardian's name, email address, and phone number (optional), and we email them the consent form.

Every participant signs a consent form (permission to attend and release of liability) before the event: a parent or guardian for participants under 18, or the participant if 18 or older. We collect the signer's name, relationship to the participant, email address, phone number, typed signature, and the date, time, IP address, and browser used to sign. We keep the signed form as the record of permission and email a copy to the signer.

We use registration details only to run the event: confirming your seat, sending venue and schedule updates, checking you in, and following up about WYMIS programs.

We use inquiry information only to respond to your inquiry, discuss bringing WYMIS to your students, and keep a record of that conversation. We do not sell, rent, or trade it, and we do not add you to a marketing list without your permission.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request, and the time of the request. These logs are used to operate and secure the site.

We may use a web analytics service to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed. If we do, this policy will name the service and explain how to opt out.

Services that help run this site

  • Hostinger hosts the website and delivers the email sent from the inquiry form, including the confirmation emails sent to you (and, for registrants under 18, to a parent or guardian).
  • Google Sheets keeps a private backup copy of each inquiry and registration so no request is lost if an email fails. Only the WYMIS team can open it.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Unsplash supplies some photographs. Your browser loads them from Unsplash's servers, which receive your IP address.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and registration emails and their backup records for as long as needed to respond, run the event, and maintain a record of our relationship with you or your school or organization. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by emailing info@wymisworks.org. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, and we will honor requests made under them.

Young people

WYMIS serves young people ages 16 to 26. Registrants who are 16 or 17 must provide a parent or guardian's contact details, and the parent or guardian must sign the consent form before the event. A parent or guardian can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete their child's registration. This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

The site uses HTTPS encryption, and inquiries and registrations are delivered by email to a private mailbox. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you send us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@wymisworks.org.
