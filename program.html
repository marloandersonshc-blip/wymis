The Program

A structured thirteenth grade for real life.

WYMIS is an 8 to 12 week, cohort-based program that teaches the practical skills students need right after graduation.

The gap

Academic credentials without everyday skills.

Most students graduate high school without many of the practical skills needed to manage money, hold a job, communicate professionally, and navigate adult life with confidence.

WYMIS takes the real-world lessons that traditional schooling often leaves out and turns them into a structured, cohort-based learning experience. It is designed for graduating seniors and young adults preparing to enter the workforce, start a business, or live independently for the first time.

The WYMIS Model

Four principles shape every cohort.

Groups move through a shared program together rather than through isolated, one-off lessons.

Cohort-based

Students move through the program together as a group, which builds accountability, peer support, and a sense of shared progress.

8 to 12 weeks

The full program is sized to fit a semester and is ideally suited for ages 16 to 26.

Skills-first

Every module is built around real situations students will face immediately after graduation.

Flexible delivery

Run any module as a focused 75-minute class session or a deeper 3-day unit with role-play and applied projects.

How a cohort works

Start together. Practice together. Finish ready.

Weeks 1 to 12

A shared program

A group of students begins WYMIS together and moves through the modules as a cohort over 8 to 12 weeks.

Each module

Learn, then practice

Modules run as a focused 75-minute session or a 3-day unit with role-play, group work, and applied projects.

After graduation

Ready for what is next

Students leave with practical confidence for a first job, a first apartment, college, or a business of their own.

Delivery formats

Two ways to run every module.

Deliver modules individually or combine them into a full cohort experience.

75minutes

Single-class session

A focused introduction to one module that fits inside a standard class period or workshop slot.

  • Core concepts in one sitting
  • Drops into an existing schedule
  • Works for advisory periods, assemblies, and events
3day unit

Applied unit

A deeper version of the module that gives students real practice time with the skill.

  • Hands-on role-play
  • Group work
  • Applied projects
Who WYMIS is for

Ages 16 to 26, and the organizations that serve them.

  • Graduating high school seniors preparing for a first job, first apartment, or college
  • Young adults entering the workforce who want practical skills schools often do not teach
  • Schools, community organizations, and workforce programs looking for a ready-to-use, real-world curriculum
