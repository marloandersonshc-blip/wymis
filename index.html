What is WYMIS

A diploma proves you finished school. It doesn’t prove you’re ready for what comes next.

WYMIS, short for What You Missed In School, is a practical thirteenth grade for workforce readiness and life skills.

It closes a gap most students experience firsthand: graduating with academic credentials but without the everyday skills needed to manage money, hold a job, communicate professionally, and navigate adult life with confidence.

The program turns the real-world lessons traditional schooling leaves out into a structured experience for graduating seniors and young adults preparing to enter the workforce, start a business, or live independently for the first time.

How the program works
Cohort-based learning
The WYMIS Model

Built for accountability, not one-off lessons.

Students move through WYMIS together. Shared progress creates peer support and keeps everyone accountable from the first session to the last.

Cohort-based

Students move through the program together as a group, which builds accountability, peer support, and a sense of shared progress.

8 to 12 weeks

The full program is sized to fit a semester and is ideally suited for ages 16 to 26.

Skills-first

Every module is built around real situations students will face immediately after graduation.

Flexible delivery

Run any module as a focused 75-minute class session or a deeper 3-day unit with role-play and applied projects.
