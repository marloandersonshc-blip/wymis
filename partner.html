Partner With WYMIS

Bring WYMIS to your community.

Schools, community organizations, and workforce programs anywhere in the country can bring WYMIS to their students as a One-Day Intensive, a 3-Day Applied Unit, or a full cohort.

Ways to partner

Choose the format that fits your students.

1 day

One-Day Intensive

A focused, full-day deep dive that condenses the core modules into one immersive day, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

  • Six 75-minute module sessions
  • Built for groups of 20 to 30
  • Ideal for a first introduction
See the schedule
3 days

3-Day Applied Unit

A hands-on, project-based unit that gives students real practice time with a skill.

  • Role-play and group work
  • Applied projects
  • Go deep on one module
8 to 12 weeks

8 to 12 Week Cohort

The full WYMIS program: all seven modules, delivered to a cohort that moves through them together.

  • All seven modules
  • Peer accountability
  • The complete thirteenth grade
How the cohort works

Need something shorter? Any single module can also run as a 75-minute session inside a class period or event.

Hosting a One-Day Intensive? See the facility checklist. Longer term, WYMIS is growing toward a licensed model organizations can run in their own communities.
